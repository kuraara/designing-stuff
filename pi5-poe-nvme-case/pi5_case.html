Pi 5 PoE NVMe Case

A slide-in tube for a Raspberry Pi 5 with the Waveshare PoE HAT (G) on its header and a Pimoroni NVMe Base Duo underneath. No screws or standoffs in either board: each board rests on its own ledge, the PCIe ribbon is the only link between them, and a click-in PLA lid closes the SD end: its tray carries the Duo from below and two corner posts stop both boards against the closed USB end. The body prints standing on its USB end, so the ledges, vents and ports need no supports.

Changes, 13 Nov

  • The SSDs sit on top of the Duo, under the Pi (your correction). The bay under the Duo that was kept for them is free.
  • Lid v0.5, PLA: a tray under the Duo. It's slotted, 2 mm thick, and reaches 63.8 mm under the Duo (75 %). Webs run underneath to just above the floor. Two corner posts put a small lip over the Duo's top corners and stop both boards. It replaces the TPU card retainer, and the two can't be fitted together.
  • Feet v0.5: M4 × 10 button heads straight into the PLA. The 4 mm floor holes were drawn for M3 heat-set inserts, and an M4 cuts its own thread in them. The feet are 3 mm taller, at 9 mm, with a Ø7.6 × 3.8 counterbore. The screw stops 0.6 mm short of the end of the hole.
  • Next body: a much wider Duo ledge (your note). It can also lose the empty bay under the Duo.
  • To print: the lid and the feet. The body stays as printed. Earlier: on 11 Nov the body printed well and the ledges held the Duo through a shake test. The TPU lid's snap bumps didn't print, which led to the PLA latch lid (v0.3).

Settled

  • SSDs: Samsung 980 series, on top of the Duo. Single-sided, 2.38 mm, no heatsink. On Pimoroni's 5 mm SSD standoffs they stand about 7.4 mm above the Duo, in the 12 mm gap under the Pi.
  • HAT height: unknown, so kept at 16 mm. That's the tall case. If your HAT sits lower, you just get more air above it.
  • HAT stays on its headers. No hold-down ribs.
  • SD tray dropped. Fit the microSD card before the PCIe ribbon.

The print-ready STLs are in the chat: body (PETG or PLA), lid (PLA), four feet (TPU), DIN bracket (PETG). Each is already oriented for the bed.

Assembly
  1. Fit the SSDs to the top of the Duo and the ribbon to both boards as Pimoroni describe, but leave the board standoffs out. Plug the HAT onto the Pi's GPIO and PoE headers. Fit your microSD card now if you use one.
  2. Fold the Duo under the Pi and slide both in from the open end, USB first: the Duo onto the lower ledges, the Pi onto the upper ledges. Push them together by their SD edges until they stop on the end wall.
  3. Push the lid in. The tray slides under the Duo (its tip lifts the board if it sags), the posts' small lips pass over the Duo's top corners, and both latches click into the upper side windows. The posts stop both boards.
  4. Screw the TPU feet on, counterbore toward the desk, with M4 × 10 button heads straight into the floor holes. They cut their own thread, so go gently the first time, and stop when the head is snug.
  5. To open, pull the lid out by the thumb notch at the top. If it's tight, press both hooks in through the side windows with a small screwdriver as you pull. The boards then slide out by their SD edges.
Printing
  • Body: USB end flat on the bed, PETG. Every wall, the lip and the drive-bay walls are vertical in that orientation. Vent slots and windows only bridge 3–10 mm. No supports, no brim.
  • Lid: outer face on the bed, PLA, no supports, no brim. Layers are 0.20 mm up to 9 mm, where the latch hooks are, then 0.30 mm. The tray, webs and posts stand straight up, about 83 mm tall, and each layer stays inside the one below. The arm slots go right through the plate; check they're clear on the first layer. The hooks' undersides are 45° overhangs.
  • Feet: TPU, counterbore side up (no overhang), printed one at a time. Turn them over to fit.
  • The click is set by latch_t (1.6, arm thickness) and latch_hook (1). Too stiff: thin the arm to 1.2. Too loose: raise the hook to 1.2. Either way, reprint only the lid (2h 03m).
Hardware
  • 4 × M4 × 10 button head screws (feet), straight into the PLA. No inserts.
  • DIN mount instead of feet (not yet changed for M4): 4 × M3 × 20 button or socket head screws into M3 heat-set inserts, TS35 rail (35 × 7.5 or 35 × 15)
  • Nothing for the boards. The Duo kit's standoffs and screws aren't used.
